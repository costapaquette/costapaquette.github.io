About ItisScientific

Independent research in regional Australia

Based in Pinnaroo, ItisScientific brings together laboratory instrumentation, industrial equipment and a renewable-powered site to investigate practical technologies. Our work spans fuels, chemical processing, materials science and related engineering challenges.

We believe credible innovation comes from careful experimentation, transparent evidence and collaboration across disciplines.
