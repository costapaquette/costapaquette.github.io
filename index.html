Research areas

Interdisciplinary research with practical purpose.

We are exploring technologies at the intersection of energy, chemistry, materials and engineering, with an emphasis on experimental evidence and industrial relevance.

01 / RENEWABLE FUELS

Ethanol & sustainable energy

Investigating ethanol production, feedstock constraints and alternative chemical pathways for renewable fuels. Current work includes fermentation planning and assessment of longer-term non-food feedstocks.

02 / APPLIED CHEMISTRY

Materials science & characterisation

Experimental research in chemical processes and materials behaviour, supported by analytical techniques and laboratory instrumentation.

03 / INDUSTRIAL SYSTEMS

Robotics & automation

Investing in robotics, automation and instrumentation to support reproducible research and future industrial applications.

04 / SOLAR SYSTEMS

Renewable-powered laboratory

Integrating on-site solar generation and energy storage with a practical research and development environment.

05 / PROCESS RESEARCH

Chemical conversion pathways

Evaluating the chemical, energy and commercial feasibility of new routes to useful fuels, feedstocks and advanced materials.

06 / COLLABORATION

Industry & research partnerships

Open to enquiries from researchers, engineers, technical suppliers and organisations interested in responsible applied research and pilot-scale feasibility.

Renewable energy programme

From sunlight to useful chemical energy.

Our operating solar and battery installation is a significant step toward powering practical chemical research with locally generated electricity.

The longer-term vision explores whether renewable electricity can support economically realistic fuel and materials pathways, while rigorously evaluating feedstock supply, energy efficiency and environmental performance.

Our story

Independent innovation from Pinnaroo.

ItisScientific (also written Itis Scientific or Itisscientific) is an independent Australian research and development business. Its renewable-energy ambitions have developed over more than two decades. Today, the company is bringing together an industrial site, scientific equipment, solar infrastructure and multidisciplinary experience to investigate technologies that can be tested, measured and assessed for real-world viability.
