Surface-dependent responses of poly(lactic acid)

Current doctoral research investigates the spectroscopically distinct surfaces of commercial PLA films and their responses to water and selected ionic-liquid treatments. The evidence base includes Raman and ATR-FTIR spectroscopy, supported by additional analytical work.
