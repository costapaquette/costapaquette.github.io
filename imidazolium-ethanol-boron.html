ItisScientific · Original research direction · Conceptual stage

Imidazolium. Ethanol. Elemental boron.

ItisScientific is evaluating a three-component propulsion research concept that brings together imidazolium-based ionic liquids, ethanol and elemental boron. The proposed combination reflects the founder's longstanding work in ionic liquids and renewable fuels alongside later interest in producing boron-containing research materials.

A research hypothesis, not a qualified or demonstrated rocket propellant

Three strands of the research concept

The proposed combination concerns three broad materials categories. Listing them together does not establish chemical compatibility, stability, practical propulsion performance or a complete propellant system.

Research strand 01

Imidazolium ionic liquids

A family of organic ionic compounds studied in materials science and, in selected forms, propulsion research. Properties depend strongly on the exact chemistry; imidazolium is not one single fuel or a guarantee of energetic behaviour.

Research strand 02

Ethanol

An established combustible liquid that can be produced from biological feedstocks. ItisScientific has investigated ethanol manufacturing and holds a documented Australian excise-licensing history dating to 2009. Renewable sourcing does not by itself prove life-cycle sustainability.

Research strand 03

Elemental boron

A high-energy-density element that has attracted propulsion research interest, although practical use is constrained by ignition and combustion-completeness challenges. The separately proposed BBr3-related laboratory work concerns possible boron production research: boron tribromide is not interchangeable with elemental boron.

Development status (November 2026): The combined three-component concept is at the proposal and literature-review stage on this website. No verified combined-formulation tests, rocket-engine firings, performance data, stability validation, commercial production or independently confirmed novelty are claimed. The three listed materials alone do not define a functioning rocket propulsion system.

Historical development of the idea

Correspondence to Southern Mallee District Council in October 2023 and October 2024 explicitly discussed imidazolium derivatives and the investigation of ethanol for advanced propulsion research. Subsequent research discussions developed interest in adding elemental boron as a further conceptual strand and examining boron-related chemistry.

The founder's work in ionic liquids also includes doctoral polymer research. Those experiments are a separate evidence base and must not be interpreted as testing an ethanol–imidazolium–boron propellant.

ItisScientific's existing ATO correspondence addresses the suitability of its excise licence for the described ethanol research. It does not confer blanket permission to manufacture, store or test experimental rocket propellants. Read the documented licensing history.
