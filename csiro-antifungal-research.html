Documented research heritage · CSIRO Clayton · 2016–2018

Spiroborates and antifungal chemistry at CSIRO.

Between 2016 and 2018, Constantinos (Costa) Arvanitis participated in chemistry research associated with CSIRO Manufacturing at Clayton under the supervision of Peter Duggan. His work included the synthesis and characterisation of boron-containing spiroborates and collaborative assessment of their antifungal activity.

Research association spanning three calendar years · 2017 interruption documented
Appointment and research record

A research programme supported by contemporary correspondence.

The original correspondence distinguishes the initial industrial traineeship from the later postgraduate studentship. It does not establish three uninterrupted years of paid CSIRO employment.

2016 · START

Industrial traineeship

CSIRO recruitment correspondence from February 2018 records the founder's statement that he began industrial training with Peter Duggan in 2016. A separate April 2016 email refers to his CSIRO office contact.

2017 · INTERRUPTION

Research contact maintained

The 2018 return correspondence records a year of absence in 2017. November 2017 emails show Peter Duggan reserving laboratory space and setting aside compounds for antifungal testing.

2018 · FORMAL STUDENTSHIP

CSIRO student affiliate

A CSIRO postgraduate studentship offer was issued on 6 February 2018. November 2018 CSIRO correspondence confirms the affiliate's final recorded appointment date was 30 November 2018.

Appointment accuracy: The documented positions are described as an industrial traineeship and subsequent postgraduate studentship/affiliate engagement. The record supports work across 2016–2018, with an interruption in 2017; it does not justify calling the whole period continuous employment.

Experimental chemistry

Compounds made, measured and assessed.

CHEMICAL SYNTHESIS

Three spiroborate products

In a laboratory update of 10 May 2018, the founder reported preparing three pure spiroborate compounds and working toward a further compound for subsequent assays. Subsequent messages discuss characterisation and ongoing synthetic work.

BIOLOGICAL ASSESSMENT

Antifungal activity

July 2018 correspondence with Peter Duggan records measured inhibitory responses, control plots and assay discussions involving Candida albicans and Candida krusei, including consideration of fluconazole as a comparator.

SCIENTIFIC COLLABORATION

Chemistry and microbiology

The programme connected chemical work at CSIRO with antifungal testing and discussions involving Professor Ian Macreadie at RMIT. Contemporary correspondence preserves the relationship between synthesised compounds, assays and interpretation.

The available messages confirm the research activities, but numerical potency claims should be assessed against the original assay records and controls before being presented as validated comparative results.
