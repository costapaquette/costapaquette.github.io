The V8 Bedford
ice-cream truck.

A distinctive mobile food project built around a classic Bedford ice-cream truck, a founder-reported V8 engine and homemade soft-serve ice cream—bringing together transport, food entrepreneurship and the research ambition of operating a generator on renewable ethanol.

The Bedford is part of the business's long-running practical development history. It has also been envisaged as a memorable platform for shows, private functions, corporate events and public science demonstrations.

A joined-up project

Food. Energy. Transport.

The truck provides a practical and public-facing example of ItisScientific's aim of connecting scientific research to useful commercial activities.

01 / FOOD

Homemade soft serve

Founder-developed soft-serve ice cream and a long history of food formulation and commercial demonstrations. Costa's earlier Big M soft-serve project was sold at the 1985 Royal Melbourne Show, according to his contemporary retrospective account.

02 / ENERGY

Ethanol-generator programme

Research aimed at using renewable ethanol to provide on-board electrical power. Correspondence to the ATO in 2015, 2017 and 2021 documents this objective; generator rating, fuel testing and current operating performance require technical confirmation.

03 / TRANSPORT & EVENTS

Classic Bedford presence

The vehicle was described as a Bedford ice-cream truck in a June 2019 transport quotation. The V8 configuration is reported by the founder. Shows, private parties, corporate functions and educational events are proposed uses, subject to availability and relevant approvals.

The historical record

Years of documented planning and development.

  1. 2 February 2015 · ATO research and business plan

    A written response discussed the ice-cream van as an application for ethanol-fuelled generation and mobile public education.

  2. 9 October 2017 · Excise licensing correspondence

    A renewed statement of the plan to power the ice-cream van's generator using ethanol produced through the business.

  3. 17 June 2019 · Bedford transport quotation

    A freight quotation between Pinnaroo and Keysborough listed the cargo as an “icecream truck”, model “bedford”, with a stated weight of approximately 2,200 kg.

  4. 11 February 2021 · ATO correspondence

    The founder again referred to an ice-cream truck as an intended application of the ethanol-fuel research programme.

  5. 29 November 2024 · Proposed local energy demonstration

    The truck and its generator were included in a broader proposed renewable-ethanol demonstration for Pinnaroo.

  6. 23 March 2025 · Pinnaroo Show expression of interest

    The founder proposed presenting an ice-cream truck alongside renewable-fuel and robotics exhibits, conditional on the vehicle being ready and satisfying health requirements. This email does not establish that the exhibition proceeded.

Vehicle and food-trading history

Previously registered; historical records being recovered.

The founder confirms that the Bedford was registered with VicRoads in Victoria for many years as an ice-cream van and carried the associated compulsory third-party personal-injury insurance included with Victorian registration.

Original Bedford-specific VicRoads registration notices and the historic local health or food-business registration certificates have not yet been recovered from the available correspondence. Previous vehicle registration and food-business approval are different records; historical approval should not be taken to demonstrate current legal readiness to trade.

Accuracy note: The documents establish the vehicle's Bedford identity and the proposed renewable-ethanol application. The historical Victorian registration and V8 configuration are founder-reported. Generator capacity and current ethanol operating status remain to be substantiated. No original photograph of the Bedford has yet been verified for publication on this page.
