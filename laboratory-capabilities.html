Laboratory infrastructure • independent research

Practical research backed by laboratory capabilities.

ItisScientific's Pinnaroo research base has been developed around scientific glassware, analytical instrumentation and specialist equipment for chemistry, polymer research and renewable-energy investigations.

Research facilities • technical inventory under continuing development

Laboratory equipment and glassware

The company's research equipment inventory includes an extensive assortment of general and specialist scientific glassware supporting small-scale chemical preparation, separation, analysis and experimental evaluation.

Scientific glassware

Laboratory vessels and fittings, reaction and distillation glassware, condensers, measuring equipment and supporting accessories for experimental chemistry. Suitability for any specific process depends on material compatibility, integrity and risk assessment.

Materials characterisation

Microscopy and analytical instrumentation used or acquired for spectroscopy, chemical assessment and examination of polymer and other research materials. The founder's PLA work includes Raman and infrared spectroscopy.

Thermal and processing equipment

Specialist ovens, furnaces, autoclaves, fume hoods and vacuum-related laboratory infrastructure form part of the company's described equipment collection. Equipment is not represented as certified for every proposed process.

Separation and analysis

Chromatographic and associated analytical equipment support investigation of research products and solutions. Individual instruments, commissioning status and performance are assessed for each research programme.

Quality and safety: An equipment inventory is not equivalent to an accredited testing laboratory, a certified production process or a regulatory approval. Materials, experiments, infrastructure and operating authorisations are evaluated individually.

Connected research infrastructure

Laboratory capabilities complement renewable-energy development, the scientific paper archive, computer-assisted analysis, robotics and the long-running ethanol research programme. Future content will include original photos of the actual equipment and documented experiments rather than generic images presented as company facilities.
