About the founder

The people and purpose behind ItisScientific.

An independent Australian research enterprise shaped by experimental science, renewable-energy ambitions and hands-on engineering.

Research profile

Constantinos (Costa) Arvanitis

Founder and director of ItisScientific · BAppSc (Honours) · PhD candidate

From scientific research to practical systems

Costa's work brings together laboratory research, materials characterisation, renewable fuel concepts and the practical challenges of building independent research infrastructure. His doctoral research investigates how water and ionic-liquid treatments affect the spectroscopic and structural behaviour of polylactic acid (PLA), using Raman and infrared spectroscopy among other analytical methods.

Australian Society for Microbiology recognition · February 2015

Costa's public professional profile records recognition by the Australian Society for Microbiology as Top Undergraduate Microbiology Student in February 2015. This recognition belongs alongside his laboratory and academic research history.

Microbiology, academic recognition and yeast research

Costa worked with Professor Ian Macreadie at RMIT in honours microbiology research and published studies using yeast systems. His professional record includes a 2015 Australian Society for Microbiology Top Undergraduate Microbiology Student distinction. This experience informs ItisScientific's planned Saccharomyces-based fuel ethanol programme using 1,500 kg of recently purchased sugar. Read the microbiology and fermentation history →

Azolla bioengineering and renewable fuels

The original 2019 doctoral research proposal examined Azolla and its cyanobacterial symbiont as possible sources of hydrogen, ethylene and ethanol. Its acetylene-to-ethylene nitrogenase link remains a biochemical research question, with original cultivation records. Read the Azolla programme →

RMIT plant biotechnology and bioethanol research

From 2012, Costa volunteered in Dr Gregory Nugent's RMIT Plant Biotechnology Research Laboratory on ARC-supported biofuels research involving transgenic plants and recombinant cellulase expression. Their subsequent scientific discussions included plant growth regulators, biological assays, rooting gel and prospective doctoral and commercial research. ItisScientific's 2017 Stay-Alive plant trials extend this history. Explore the documented research timeline →

Commercial invention before the modern research enterprise

The founder's product-development record predates his present laboratory programmes. His 2015 correspondence records profitable sales of a Big M soft-serve ice-cream concept first exhibited at the Royal Melbourne Show in 1985, a proprietary Mirro Majik mirror paint associated with a historical provisional patent filing, and later soy-based soft-serve ice cream. The invention and product history distinguishes the founder's own products from the original Big M trademark and documents the sources and their limits.

A regional research base

The research site in Pinnaroo, South Australia, has been developed around analytical and chemical-processing equipment, a three-phase electrical supply and on-site solar and battery infrastructure. The aim is to evaluate chemical pathways against actual measurements, energy requirements, safety obligations and economic feasibility.

Renewable fuels

Research interests include ethanol production through fermentation, alternative carbon feedstocks and the scientific assessment of a possible calcium-carbide-to-ethanol route. These are development programmes, not claims of commercially demonstrated production.

Scientific collaboration with Professor Ian Macreadie

Research collaboration with Professor Ian Macreadie at RMIT contributed to the founder's two published peer-reviewed works, including the 2020 review of boron-containing antifungal mechanisms. Correspondence from March–April 2019 documents Professor Macreadie's involvement in the editorial proofs and final publication approval. Read the publication record →

Published scientific work

Costa is first author of a 2020 peer-reviewed review on boron-containing antifungal compounds and co-author of a 2016 article on amyloid-beta research using yeast models. The publications page links directly to the publisher and PubMed records, and separates these completed works from current research.

Research integrity

ItisScientific distinguishes established chemistry from experimental hypotheses, planned engineering from commissioned equipment, and regulatory permissions from broader claims of endorsement. Results will be described on the basis of documented measurements and reviewed evidence.
