Developing a private scientific AI assistant

A long-term aim is to build a locally controlled AI-assisted research environment that helps scientists search references, compare findings and reason over independently prepared scientific notes, original experimental data and material that is permitted for machine use.

1. Structured research records

Bibliographic identifiers, citations, original research notes, factual datasets and summaries are indexed so each finding remains traceable to a source.

2. Evidence-aware retrieval

A retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) system is a potential first stage, enabling a local AI model to find relevant evidence without training its underlying weights on every article PDF.

3. Models trained on eligible material

Private experiments may use original scientific data, independently authored material, open-licensed datasets and publications where the applicable terms permit model training.

4. Scientific validation

Model answers are checked against source records and experimental measurements. Reported results should clearly distinguish observation, inference and hypothesis.

Computing investment and future capacity

Computer and workstation infrastructure has been a multi-year planning and procurement focus. Correspondence in 2023 records acquisition of a 24-core AMD Threadripper Pro processor and an ASUS WRX80 workstation motherboard; memory expansion and a future NVIDIA A100-class accelerator were under consideration. A fully configured 2 TB system or A100 installation is not claimed as established by these records.

Future development will favour private storage, reproducible scientific workflows and a clear rights register for research sources.

Responsible publication use: Access to a copyrighted article, including a purchased copy, does not automatically include permission to redistribute it or use it for model training. ItisScientific intends to use original factual summaries and datasets, licensed content, and tools that respect publisher terms and applicable Australian copyright requirements. The original PDFs are not offered for public download.
